RAISE AI Risk & Carbon Footprint Calculator

Early Risk Rating Scoring Tool for AI Deployment

Dual Scoring: Risk Management & Environmental Impact

📋 Use Case & AI Assessment

AI System Types: Generative AI, Agentic AI, and General Purpose AI Systems require enhanced governance and risk management approaches.
Individual Interaction: Systems interacting with individuals require transparency disclosures and additional safeguards.

🛡️ Governance & Security

Privacy Risk: Higher privacy risks require enhanced governance controls.
Security Level: Poor security increases operational risk significantly.
AI Safety: Poor alignment increases risk of unintended consequences.
Regulatory: Non-compliance can result in severe penalties and deployment blocks.

🌱 Carbon Footprint Assessment

Deployment Type: In-house builds allow more control over carbon optimization.
Model Size: Larger models require more computational resources and energy.
Training Frequency: More frequent training increases energy consumption.
Infrastructure: Green energy sources significantly reduce carbon footprint.
Usage Volume: Higher inference volumes increase overall energy consumption.

Score Interpretation Guide

Risk Score Ranges
0 - 6 LOW RISK: Standard controls sufficient
6 - 12 MEDIUM RISK: Enhanced monitoring required
12 - 20 HIGH RISK: Extensive controls needed
20+ CRITICAL: Deployment blocked
Carbon Impact Ranges
0 - 2.5 LOW IMPACT: Minimal environmental footprint
2.5 - 3.5 MODERATE: Optimization recommended
3.5 - 4.5 HIGH IMPACT: Immediate action needed
4.5+ CRITICAL: Redesign required
